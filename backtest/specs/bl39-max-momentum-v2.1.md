BL-39 MAX momentum v2.1 (frozen)
Hypothesis: among established, liquid Base tokens, those with the largest one-day jump in the last 30 days beat the average token of the same universe over the next 7 days, and the long side makes money after the cost of trading it and beats holding ETH over the same legs.
DATA: backtest/liquid/ from b1b6113 (daily candles of each token's deepest pool, every day ended before CUT 2026-11-08T00:00Z). Candles = GeckoTerminal daily candles in USD on UTC days; volume = the deepest pool's own USD volume. ETH = GeckoTerminal daily candles (token=WETH) of the deepest WETH/USDC pool on Base, Uniswap v3 0x6c561b446416e1a00e8e93e221854d6ea4171372 (the pool of BL-33's eth-<day> files, tools/candles.py eth_pool()), USD, UTC days, fetched once, nothing after CUT. Pool fee tiers as read at Base block 59,957,430, from the committed per-pool file backtest/specs/bl39-max-momentum-v2.1-fees.json (pool, dex, fee, may_differ, unknown, route hops to USDC), sha256 4a7318c95a9650f5fa533ba0f9ac552db0b0449d7a9e8e22bca6797b16991180. FAMILY LIST = backtest/specs/bl39-max-momentum-v2.1-family.json, sha256 374b70eaf450e03d6a2bc208708fa1d3afcdaad77d080c2639aa39a00becc9ab.
KNOWN BIASES, accepted and stated: the token list is the Nov 9 top lists (survivors only); the deepest pool is the Nov 9 one; 129 tokens the desk saw on Oct 23 - Nov 7 were dropped; fee tiers are from Nov 20 and may differ, either way, in Jun-Oct for the 32 of 64 non-family pools whose fee can change (BRETT's moved 0.2102% -> 0.37% in one week); the asset filter (stable / wrapped / quote) used each token's Nov 7 close and Nov 9 pair counts; one May candle row was seen in BL-48 (accepted, DECISIONS 2026-11-20). The survivor list and the Nov 9 pool favour the long side and also the high-MAX picks over the benchmark, with no upper bound (NOTES "BL-39 survey"). A fail is strong evidence, a pass is weak: a pass means "not killed", never "proven".
DECISION TIME = each Monday 00:00 UTC (m).
UNIVERSE at m (a missing number = not in): universe.json tokens only (stables, wrapped and quote tokens already removed by the survey); deepest pool created at or before m - 31 d (the first of the 31 candles is a full day, never the launch day); all 31 daily candles in [m - 31 d, m); median daily USD volume of the deepest pool over [m - 30 d, m) >= 50,000; that pool's USD volume >= 10,000 on every day in [m - 7 d, m); EXCLUDED: the ADS family = the 39 token addresses in FAMILY LIST (full 40-hex, frozen: the 22 ADS-paired tokens of tokens.json + the 17 in NOTES "BL-39 pre-freeze"). They are the owner's rule (DECISIONS 2026-11-20) applied once to the 103 union tokens: GoPlus creator_address = 0xd95a366a2c887033ba71743c6342e2df470e9db9, or the deepest pool's other token is ADS 0xb20a4bd059f5914a2f8b9c18881c637f79efb7df; a token with no GoPlus creator answer is a member only if its deepest pool is ADS-quoted; plus ADS itself (not in universe.json, removes nothing). The run uses the list, never a live GoPlus call; the code asserts that the rule gives exactly the list.
SIGNAL: MAX = the largest of the 30 close-to-close daily returns in [m - 31 d, m).
PICKS: k = ceil(n / 10), n = the universe size at m. The k eligible universe tokens with the highest MAX; ties by lower address (lowercase hex). A token stopped in the week before m stays in the universe, in n and in the benchmark, but is not eligible at m; its slot goes to the next eligible token by MAX.
LEG: one pick for one week. It starts at the open of the daily candle that starts at m (no candle that day = no_fill, counted) and ends at the open of the candle that starts at m + 7 d (if missing, the open of the first candle in the next 3 days; else the close of the leg's last candle = exit_gap). For stage 1's last Monday no later open is searched: a missing 2026-09-07 candle = exit_gap. A leg that ends by exit, stop, roll or exit_gap is a closed leg; a no_fill is not a leg.
ROLL: a token picked again at m + 7 d in the same stage is not sold: its leg ends and the next begins at the open of the candle that starts at m + 7 d, with no friction on either side. If that candle is missing, the old leg ends by the exit_gap rule (a real sell) and the new pick is a no_fill. No roll across a stage end: a stage's last-Monday legs end with a real sell; each stage is its own 50 USD book.
STOP (fixed before entry): -30% from the leg's starting price (the entry fill for a new position, the open for a rolled one), checked on every daily candle of the leg, the first day included. A low at or below it exits at the lowest of the stop level, that day's open and that day's close.
TICKET 5 USD per leg. A leg's net P&L = 5 USD x its net return, rolled legs included (as if re-sized to 5 USD at no cost), booked on the day the leg ends.
FRICTION per real buy or sell: the fee tiers of the route from USDC (the deepest pool plus each hop, by BL-45's hop rule) + 0.5%. Unknown fee (as NOTES "BL-39 pre-freeze" defines it: hook-set per swap or unreadable) = 1%. No impact term; gas is inside the 0.5%.
BENCHMARK: at each m, one leg for every universe token (tokens stopped the week before included), same LEG, ROLL and STOP rules, gross, equal weight. A week with no closed pick leg counts as P_w <= B_w.
HOLD-ETH: per leg, ETH's daily open on the leg's start day to ETH's daily open on its end day (that day's close for a stop, or for an exit_gap that ends at a last close), no friction.
STAGE 1 = Mondays 2026-06-15 .. 2026-08-31 (12), run once, results read.
STAGE 2 = Mondays 2026-09-07 .. 2026-10-26 (8), run once, ONLY if stage 1 passes. If stage 1 fails, stage 2 is never read and stays unspent.
RUN: this text is frozen as a spec file whose sha256 is recorded in DECISIONS; the tool refuses any other hash. The stage 1 run reads nothing after the open of the 2026-09-07 candle. Stage 2 runs only after a DECISIONS line records "stage 1 pass". A second run of a stage needs an owner rerun line in DECISIONS, is logged as a rerun and never replaces the first result. BL-38's spec is frozen before BL-39 stage 1 is read, or BL-38 tests only data after CUT.
PER WEEK w: P_w = mean gross return of that week's pick legs; B_w = mean gross return of that week's benchmark legs.
PASS, each stage on its own (all five):
 (1) closed legs >= 40 in stage 1, >= 30 in stage 2;
 (2) mean net P&L per leg after friction > 0, and still > 0 without the top 2 legs;
 (3) the mean of (P_w - B_w) > 0, AND P_w > B_w in at least 8 of 12 weeks (stage 1) / 6 of 8 weeks (stage 2);
 (4) mean net return per leg > hold-ETH over the same legs;
 (5) max drawdown < 30% on a 50 USD book, equity updated once per exit date with that date's summed P&L.
A stage that fails 2, 3 or 4 is retired, whatever its leg count. A stage that would otherwise pass but has fewer legs than (1) is inconclusive = not passed; if it is stage 1, stage 2 is not run and stays unspent. No changed spec is ever tested on stage 1's weeks. Only 5 fails = not passed: drawdown: stage 2 is not run; forward paper vs the benchmark, never money. Both stages pass = forward paper vs the benchmark, never money. Forward paper rebuilds the universe each Monday from the BL-46 snapshots (no fixed token list).
Always reported, never the verdict: legs, distinct tokens, legs per token, share of legs that are rolls, no_fill, exit_gap, stops; mean net without the top 2 legs; results per week and by ETH 7-day return before m (>= 0 / < 0); friction at half and at double; the BL-45 measured-cost line; v1's line (4% a side, re-buy every week); picks that joined the universe in the last 7 days; P_w - B_w split by ETH's return over the leg week (>= 0 / < 0); B_w without the 0.01-0.05% pools; B_w over the top third of the universe by 30-day volatility; picks whose MAX day is the last day before m; legs on unknown-fee, hook and changeable-fee pools, with tests 2 and 4 re-run with only those legs at double friction; exit_gap legs at -100%; picks lost to no_fill and slots refilled after a stop.
