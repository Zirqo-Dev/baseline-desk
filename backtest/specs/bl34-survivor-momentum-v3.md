BL-34 spec v3: survivor momentum (approved by the owner 2026-11-11, frozen; replaces v2)
Hypothesis: Base tokens still alive about 24 h after the desk first saw them, with real depth >= 10k USD and real sellers, that are beating ETH over the last 6 h, keep rising long enough to pay 8% round-trip friction; they beat a random pick from the same survivors and beat holding ETH.
DECISION TIME = the +24 h outcome label of a desk sighting. NO pool-age rule: any pool age.
UNIVERSE (as of the decision; a missing number = not in): +24 h label gone = 0; +24 h real depth (best_qliq, else qliq) >= 10,000 USD; +24 h pair_s24 >= 30; pool match exact, single or age.
ENTRY (all): token 6 h return > 0 (candles that ended before the decision); token 6 h return > ETH 6 h return; REGIME ETH 24 h return >= -3%.
ENTRY FILL: the open of the first candle that starts at or after t, if it starts less than 300 seconds after t; otherwise the close of the last candle that ended at or before t (an AMM pool quotes at its last price). No candle that ended at or before t = no_fill (counted). On an open fill, exits are checked from the entry candle, as in v2; on a last-close fill, from the first candle that starts after t.
ETH: a price from a candle that ended more than 10 minutes before it is needed is missing. Missing at the decision or the entry fill = the trade is not in. Missing at the exit fill = the trade is left out of test 4's pairing and counted; test 4 FAILS if more than 10% of closed trades are unpaired (on either path).
EXITS (fixed before entry): hard stop -25% (inside a candle: worse of stop and close); ladder +40% sell 1/3, +100% sell 1/3; trail 25% from the peak after the first rung, earlier candles only; max hold 72 h; NO 45-min time stop.
CLOSE-ONLY PATH: the same entries, with every candle after the entry candle (for a last-close fill: every candle that starts after t) replaced by its close (open = high = low = close); same exits, ticket and friction.
TICKET 5 USD, one trade per token. FRICTION per side: 3% + 1% pool fee on EVERY trade + impact 5 USD / qliq at +24 h.
TWIN: at each strategy entry time t, a random pick among universe tokens (universe rules only) whose +24 h decision fell in (t - 6 h, t], with a qualified candle line, entered at t by the same ENTRY FILL rule, with the same exits, ticket and friction; the twin has no ETH entry condition (no 6 h or regime rule), but the ETH line's missing-price rules apply to it as to the strategy; empty pool = twin_skip (counted).
HOLD-ETH: ETH return from each trade's entry fill to its exit fill, no friction.
SPLIT: development = decision days up to 2026-11-07; holdout = decision days 2026-11-08 .. 2026-11-21, run once.
RUN: the holdout run is refused unless the token candles and the ETH candles of every holdout decision reach decision + 72 h (+ the 5-minute fill wait); both are fetched after the earliest safe time, 2026-11-25 00:10 UTC. Every pass test is computed on that one run, and its result is the verdict. After the lock is written, a second run needs an owner holdout-rerun line in docs/DECISIONS.md, is logged under reruns and never replaces the first result; the candles used are the ones fetched before that first run.
PASS (holdout, once; all five): (1) >= 20 closed trades; (2) mean net P&L per trade after friction > 0 on the verdict path AND on the close-only path; (3) > the twin's mean net per trade; (4) mean return per trade > hold-ETH on the verdict path AND on the close-only path, each against hold-ETH over its own entry-to-exit windows; (5) max drawdown of the cumulative per-trade P&L (by exit time) on a 50 USD book < 30%. A test that fails on either path fails. Under 20 closed = inconclusive (forward paper vs twin). Fails 2, 3 or 4 = retired. Only 5 fails = not passed: drawdown (forward paper vs twin, no money). Always reported, never the verdict: no_fill rate, pending, twin_skip, stop fill "level", tick-like close-only exits, results by pool age at the decision (24-72 h, 72 h-30 d, > 30 d, unknown), the expected twin (mean net per trade over every token of each twin pool, same rules) and the strategy's rank among 1,000 seeded twin draws, the v2 no_fill line: the count and results of the trades that v2's 5-minute rule would have dropped, and the results if they were dropped, the number of twin draws equal to the strategy's own token, the age of each last-close fill (minutes since that candle ended), min / median / max, in both books.
